# Synthetic conveyor investigation guide

This guide applies only to MaintenanceAI's synthetic conveyor motor demonstration. It is not an OEM manual, a physical-machine procedure, or evidence of facility approval. Revision DEMO-2 is approved only within the simulated application.

## Hypothesis: Bearing-related vibration

An elevated vibration observation together with a compatible bearing-impulse-like model result is consistent with a possible bearing-related condition in the synthetic conveyor. This is a hypothesis, not a confirmed physical root cause. Confirming it requires qualified human investigation and approved equipment-specific evidence.

## Hypothesis: Imbalance-related vibration

A compatible imbalance-like model result accompanied by elevated vibration is consistent with a possible imbalance-related condition in the synthetic conveyor. This remains an unconfirmed hypothesis. A qualified person must compare operating-state and load evidence and follow approved isolation procedures before any physical investigation.

## Alternative: Sensor or mounting issue

A loose vibration sensor, mounting resonance or sensor-quality problem can produce abnormal vibration evidence. These remain alternative hypotheses until sensor installation and measurement quality are verified by qualified personnel under the approved isolation procedure.

## Alternative: Imbalance or process loading

An imbalance-like signal or changing process load can also contribute to elevated vibration and current. Compare operating-state and load records before interpreting a fault classification. The synthetic pattern does not prove which mechanical condition exists.

## Check: Review recorded measurements

Review the already-recorded vibration spectrum, temperature trend, current trend and operating-state timestamps. Confirm sensor quality and sampling metadata. This is a review of recorded data and does not require touching or controlling equipment.

## Check: Plan qualified inspection

A qualified maintenance manager must review an inspection proposal. Any physical inspection may occur only after personnel apply the facility-approved shutdown, isolation and verification procedures. This demonstration guide does not supply those physical-machine procedures or authorize intervention.

## Safety: Escalation and verification

Escalate critical operating-limit excursions to the responsible maintenance manager under the configured site process. Protective systems must remain enabled. No model, risk score or copilot answer authorizes machine operation, maintenance execution or control changes.
